Operational Flow

How StockFlow Works

A streamlined 4-step workflow that keeps your store shelf-ready.

1
Add Products

Register your inventory catalog with custom SKU identifiers, pricing, and minimum stock alert levels.

2
Track Stock

Record incoming supplier shipments (Stock IN) and counter dispatch sales (Stock OUT) with transactional guarantees.

3
Receive Alerts

When items drop below safety thresholds, automatic in-app alerts notify store managers before stockouts occur.

4
Better Decisions

Analyze inventory valuation, optimize reorder timing, and minimize dead stock across all departments.
